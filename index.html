Leafsong Retreat Sector MU – Resort-Inspired 3 BHK Living in Greater Noida

Leafsong Retreat by Hero Homes is a resort-inspired residential address in Sector MU, Greater Noida, offering elegantly designed 3 BHK homes from about 153.84 to 212.68 sq. mtr. The community rises across three iconic 30-storey towers above three basement levels, with a pedestrian-first masterplan, lush greenery, and a thoughtfully planned open environment. Residents enjoy an integrated clubhouse of around 2,322.58 sq. mtr. with lifestyle, wellness, and recreational amenities.
